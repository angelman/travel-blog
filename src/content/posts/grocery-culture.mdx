A main goal of this trip around the country is to learn enough about each city to determine where I want to establish my new home. This means that I have to live in each stop for long enough to really absorb the vibe, the culture, and the feel of everyday living. Before my journey, I got this pro tip from an old friend.

> To really get a feel of what it’s like to live there, go grocery shopping.

Of course I had to try this, especially since I’m not staying in Vegas for very long. Plus, I’m already running low on my supply of canned black beans. Thing is, I don’t even know what the main grocery store chain is in Las Vegas. Life would be so much easier if they were all called Safeway. Instead, I had to do a search in Google Maps and rely on pictures to guess which one looked like the main store. I think I found it.

<figure><Photo src="/media/2021/04/img_5454.jpg" alt="" priority /><figcaption>Omega Mart: You have no idea what’s in store</figcaption></figure>

A little weird that the entirety of the grocery store is inside of another, much larger building, but I don’t judge a book by its cover.

After paying an entrance fee and getting my body scanned for weapons, I went straight for the canned goods aisle, to get that out of the way.
